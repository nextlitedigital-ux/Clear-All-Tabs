Privacy Policy for Clear All Tabs

Last updated: November 2026

Thank you for choosing Clear All Tabs! This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our browser extension. Our goal is to keep tab management simple, fast, and completely private.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Clear All Tabs does not collect, track, store, or transmit your personal information to any external server.

  • We do not record, log, or upload your browsing history, open tab list, website URLs, or search queries.
  • We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, IP address, device ID, or location.
  • We do not use analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or telemetry scripts.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

2. Permissions and Local Processing

Clear All Tabs only requests browser permissions that are necessary for its core features to work properly:

  • contextMenus: Used only to add quick tab-closing options and a Settings link to your browser's right-click menu.
  • storage: Used only to save your preferences locally, such as tab-closing behavior, custom URL, and pinned tab settings.

All processing takes place locally inside your browser. Your settings stay in your browser storage, and we do not collect, access, or receive your personal data.

3. Data Retention and Your Control

Because no data is sent to external servers, we do not hold copies of your information. You remain in full control of your preferences at any time—you can change settings inside the extension or remove all stored data simply by clearing extension data or uninstalling Clear All Tabs.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect improvements to the extension or clarity in how we describe our practices. Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page with an updated date at the top.
